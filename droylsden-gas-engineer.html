Local knowledge

Working in Droylsden

Droylsden is where I'm based, so it's the area I know best and can usually reach quickest. The housing stock here is a real mix — Victorian and Edwardian terraces around the Market Street and Manchester Road areas, interwar semis toward Audenshaw, and newer estates near the Ashton Canal and Fairfield.

That mix matters for heating work. Older terraces often still have back boilers or first-generation combis in awkward positions, and pipework that's been altered several times over the years. Newer builds are typically straightforward combi installs but can suffer from undersized radiators or poorly balanced systems. Either way, I'd rather look at what's actually there than quote blind.

Postcodes covered here: M43, and the immediately surrounding M34, M35 and M11 areas.
