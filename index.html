Privacy Policy

How McNamara Heating collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Who I am

McNamara Heating is a sole-trader gas and heating engineering business based in Droylsden, Greater Manchester. Stephen McNamara is the data controller for any personal information collected through this website. Contact: info@mcnamaraheating.co.uk or 07931 729 022.

What I collect

When you submit the enquiry form, I collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, the service you're enquiring about, your preferred appointment date, and any message you choose to add. I do not collect payment details through this website.

Why I collect it

Your details are used solely to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, arrange an appointment, and carry out any work you instruct me to do — including issuing certificates such as CP12 gas safety records where applicable. I do not use your information for marketing without your separate consent.

Who sees your information

Your enquiry is sent directly to Stephen McNamara. I do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, except where legally required (for example, gas safety certificates that must be filed as part of landlord compliance) or where a trusted service provider processes it on my behalf solely to operate this website's contact form.

How long I keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as necessary to respond to you and, where a job proceeds, for as long as required for invoicing, warranty, and legal compliance records (for example, gas safety certificates, which by law must be retained for a minimum period).

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it where I am not legally required to keep it. To make a request, contact me using the details above.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Any strictly necessary technical data used to display the site is not used to identify you personally.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
