Common faults I repair

Boiler Faults I Fix Across Manchester

From pressure drops to complete breakdowns — I diagnose and repair all common boiler faults across every major brand.

  • No heating or hot waterDiverter valve, pump failure, thermostat, pressure issue
  • Boiler locks out / won't restartIgnition fault, gas pressure, flame sensor, PCB
  • Boiler losing pressureExpansion vessel, pressure relief valve, system leak
  • Strange noisesSludge build-up, pump cavitation, limescale (kettling)
  • Error codes displayedAll manufacturer fault codes diagnosed on inspection
  • Pilot light keeps going outThermocouple, draught issue, gas supply problem
  • Radiators not heating evenlySludge, balancing, zone valves, pump flow
  • Boiler leaking waterPressure relief valve, pump seal, heat exchanger
  • No hot water (heating works fine)Diverter valve, thermistor, plate heat exchanger
  • Boiler overheating / cutting outThermistor, overheat stat, scale build-up
  • Frozen condensate pipeCommon in winter — often fixable without a callout
  • Smart thermostat/control issuesWiring, pairing, system compatibility
